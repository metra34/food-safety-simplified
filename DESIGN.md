## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative regulatory expertise coupled with fresh, actionable clarity. Purpose-built for high-stakes food safety auditing, compliance operations, and technical consulting, the UI communicates immaculate hygiene, technical competence, and uncompromising procedural integrity.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Precision Modernism:** Strict geometric discipline governed by a zero-radius rule. Straight 90-degree lines and rectilinear perimeters convey audit-grade certainty and laboratory precision.
- **High-Contrast Clinical Light Mode:** Clean whites and surgical cool slates create a daylight-bright environment with maximum legibility for compliance checklists, technical documentation, and audit records.
- **Dual-Anchor Tone:** The deep institutional authority of deep brand blue provides structure and gravitas, punctuated by the vibrant bio-hygiene green for verification stamps, pass indicators, and active compliance tasks.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for clinical legibility and instant procedural recognition:

- **Primary Blue (`#003e7c`):** The foundational anchor representing regulatory authority and structural discipline. Applied across all main headings, navigation bars, brand chrome, primary button fills, and structural frames. Hover shifts deeper to `#002b57`.
- **Secondary Green (`#5fa820`):** The active compliance accent. Reserved for verified checkmarks, inspection pass stamps, affirmative badge fills, and compliance call-to-action triggers. Hover deepens to `#4d8a19`.
- **Neutral & Structural Foundations:**
  - **Pure White (`#ffffff`):** Unadorned base for data tables, inspection cards, and clear document containers.
  - **Slate Canvas (`#f4f7f9`):** Ground background layer that creates crisp contrast against nested white content surfaces without glare.
  - **Hairline Border (`#dce3ea`):** 1px structural dividing lines and container outlines.
  - **Text Primary (`#0d1c2f`):** Near-black navy ensuring maximum contrast and legibility across all data surfaces.
  - **Text Muted (`#555d69`):** Balanced mid-slate for timestamps, metadata, field hints, and column labels.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is standard across all typography levels, unifying editorial headings, technical tables, and navigational chrome.

- **Headings & Display:** Weight 700 and 800 provide crisp authority for high-level numbers, audit ratings, and section headers. Tight letter-spacing maintains compact density on high-resolution displays.
- **Body & Tabular Copy:** Weight 400 with optimized line-height ensures sustained reading comfort across audit checklists, legal specs, and operational reports.
- **Labels & Audit Badges:** Displayed in uppercase weight 700 with open letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to evoke clinical stamps, regulatory codes, and certification markers.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid provides strict architectural layout alignment:

- **Desktop Framework:** Maximum content width of `1280px` centered with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` column gutters.
- **Mobile Adaptation:** Collapses to 4 columns with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` column gutters.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** All interior component padding and layout margins increment cleanly along a 4px / 8px baseline. Dense operational tables utilize `space-sm` (`0.5rem`), standard card modules use `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), and major audit section divisions utilize `space-xl` (`2.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

To protect the clinical aesthetic, this design system avoids diffuse, blurry, or ambient shadows. Visual hierarchy and stacked levels are established strictly through hard surface contrast and architectural lines:

- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#f4f7f9`. Active cards, panels, and data surfaces rise strictly through `#ffffff` fills bounded by a sharp `1px solid #dce3ea` perimeter.
- **Hard Technical Offsets:** When elevated hierarchy is required (such as dropdown menus, focused modals, or active primary controls), use crisp zero-blur hard offsets (`3px 3px 0px #003e7c` or `2px 2px 0px #00000015`).
- **Structural Accent Borders:** High-priority cards and panels use a solid `3px` top or left indicator edge—`#003e7c` for procedural items or `#5fa820` for verified compliance status.

## Shapes

Strict zero-radius architecture (`border-radius: 0px` / `rounded-none`) is applied across all UI elements without exception:

- **Perimeters:** Buttons, cards, modals, tabs, dropdowns, input fields, badges, and notification bars must possess 90-degree sharp corners.
- **No Softened Elements:** Avatars, toggles, and icon badges must remain squarely framed to honor the clinical inspection motif.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Brand Button:** Solid `#003e7c` background, `#ffffff` text, `0px` radius, weight 700, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover: `#002b57` background with a `3px 3px 0px #388ae5` hard offset ring. Active: translated 2px down-right into the hard offset.
- **Compliance Accent Button:** Solid `#5fa820` background, `#ffffff` text, `0px` radius, weight 700, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover: `#4d8a19`.
- **Secondary Border Button:** `#ffffff` background, `2px solid #003e7c`, `#003e7c` text, `0px` radius. Hover: Solid `#003e7c` fill with `#ffffff` text.

### Badges & Compliance Tags
- **Authority Tag:** Solid `#003e7c` fill with `#ffffff` text, `0px` radius, uppercase tracking (`0.08em`), padding `0.25rem 0.6rem`.
- **Compliance Pass Badge:** Pure white `#ffffff` surface, `1.5px solid #5fa820`, text `#5fa820`, weight 700, uppercase with verified check icon.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Pure white `#ffffff` background, `1px solid #dce3ea` border, `0px` border-radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state: `1px solid #003e7c` with a high-contrast `2px solid #388ae5` sharp outline.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square containers (`0px` radius), `1.5px solid #003e7c`. Checked state: solid `#5fa820` or `#003e7c` background with a crisp white tick mark.
- **Switches:** Rectilinear sliding block design with 0px radius, strictly avoiding pill shapes.

### Cards & Service Panels
- **Inspection Card:** `#ffffff` fill, `1px solid #dce3ea` border, `0px` radius, `1.5rem` internal padding.
- **Compliance Card:** Adds a top `4px solid #5fa820` border to signal passing audit criteria or active bio-safety protocols.
